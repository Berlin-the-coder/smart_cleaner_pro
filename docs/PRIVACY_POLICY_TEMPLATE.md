**Privacy Policy for Smart Cleaner Pro**
Last updated: [DATE]

[DEVELOPER NAME / COMPANY NAME] ("we", "us") built Smart Cleaner Pro as
a free/freemium app. This page informs users of our policies regarding
the collection, use, and disclosure of information when using our app.

## Information we access on your device

To provide the app's core features, Smart Cleaner Pro requests access
to:
- **Storage / media files** — to scan for junk, duplicate, and
  compressible files. File names, sizes, and paths are read to display
  scan results. **Files and their contents are never uploaded, copied
  off your device, or transmitted to us or any third party.**
- **Installed app list** (Android only) — to power the App Manager
  feature. This list is read on-device to display app names and sizes;
  it is not transmitted off-device.
- **Battery status** — to power the Battery Monitor feature.

## Information collected by third-party services

This app uses the following third-party services, which may collect
information used to identify you:

- **Google AdMob** — for displaying ads. See Google's privacy policy:
  https://policies.google.com/privacy
- **Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase** — to process the
  "Remove Ads" purchase. We do not receive or store your payment card
  details; these are handled entirely by Google/Apple.

## Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit the contents of your files,
photos, or personal documents. All scanning happens locally on your
device.

## Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age of
digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Your choices

- You can revoke storage/media/photo permissions at any time via your
  device's system settings; the affected features will stop working
  until permission is re-granted.
- You can opt out of personalized ads via your device's ad settings
  (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking).

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are effective
immediately upon posting to this page.
