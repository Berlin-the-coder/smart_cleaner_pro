# Store Copy & Branding

## App logo suggestion

A rounded-square icon with a **rocket or broom silhouette** in white,
centered on the primary gradient (blue → teal, see palette below).
Avoid a magnifying glass or shield motif — those read as
security/antivirus apps and can mislead users about what the app does.
Keep the icon simple enough to read at 48×48 (launcher size).

## Color palette
